The Elastic AI Assistant brings an LLM directly into your observability workflow — contextualised on your own logs, metrics, and alerts, not a generic training set.

Natural language to ES|QL — describe what you want to find; the assistant writes the query
Alert interpretation — paste an alert and get a plain-language root-cause hypothesis with supporting log and metric evidence
Runbook generation — the assistant drafts remediation steps grounded in your actual environment and past resolutions
Air-gap friendly — runs entirely on self-hosted LLMs via Ollama or vLLM; no internet or cloud API access required
