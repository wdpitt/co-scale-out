"Which DataNode hosts had the most WARN or ERROR log events in the last 6 hours — group by host and show the top error message for each."
↓  AI generated ES|QL
Generated query
FROM logs-hadoop.datanode-* // last 6h via time filter | WHERE log.level IN ("WARN", "ERROR") | STATS event_count = COUNT(*), top_message = TOP(message, 1) BY host.name | SORT event_count DESC | LIMIT 10
