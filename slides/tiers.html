Tiered Storage Architecture

Right data, right tier, right cost.

Every byte automatically migrated by ILM policy — search performance and cost in balance at every point in the data lifecycle.

Tier 1
Hot
48data nodes (proposed)
18i3.8xlarge · 7.6 TB NVMe ea.
NVMe SSD
Highest IOPS, full indexing throughput. Recent data — minutes to days. Sized to absorb 40TB/day ingest without performance degradation.
Tier 2
Cold
30data nodes (proposed)
15i3en.6xlarge · 15 TB NVMe ea.
Dense NVMe
Read-optimised, high-capacity nodes. Weeks to months of searchable history. Dense NVMe maximises GB/$ at this tier without sacrificing query speed.
Tier 3
Frozen
6data nodes (proposed)
6i3en.2xlarge · 5 TB NVMe ea.
S3 + Local Cache
Long-term retention backed by S3. Data stays fully searchable — compute spins up on query, cost stays near zero at rest. Years of history, pennies per GB.
